MCraft Block Texture Pack

Put your textures in this folder.

Recommended format:
- JPG or PNG
- 16 x 16 pixels recommended (32x32 and 64x64 also supported)
- Square images only
- Pixel-art textures work best

Filename mapping:
- grass.jpg
- dirt.jpg
- stone.jpg
- sand.jpg
- leaves.jpg
- trunk.jpg
- water.jpg

For per-face textures, you may later use:
- grass_top.jpg
- grass_side.jpg
- grass_bottom.jpg

The game automatically falls back to the built-in material when a texture is missing.
